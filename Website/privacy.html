Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

LookFar does not collect or sell personal data. There is no account, advertising, analytics, tracking SDK, developer-operated server, or in-app network request. No camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, health data, actual screen use, or eye movement is accessed or monitored.

Preferences, the current timer, and user-completed break records remain in this app's storage on your device. Your system backup settings may include this data in backups. Local notifications and Live Activities use Apple's on-device system features. Timer information may appear on the Lock Screen; visibility is controlled in iOS Settings.

Reset all data in the app deletes reminders, the timer, history and preferences. Uninstalling also removes local app data. Manage existing backups separately.

If you choose to email support, your email address and message are transmitted through email providers to the developer. Please do not include sensitive data.
